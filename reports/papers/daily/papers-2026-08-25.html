英文原文

Speech interfaces put ASR in front of retrieval, so the query a RAG system sees is already corrupted. We test whether the standard multi-hop methods, entity-graph linking and iterative reformulation, absorb that corruption or amplify it. The findings suggest that they amplify it. The structurally richer configurations generally reach higher absolute F1 under ASR input, but they give up more of their clean-text advantage: the gap from clean text to the highest-WER accent is 36-67% larger under IRCoT+HippoRAG2 than under naive dense retrieval, on all three benchmarks. Corruption of one or more query entities is the dominant failure mode, accounting for 87-96% of degradation cases on 2WikiMultiHopQA across all four methods. Two lightweight surface-form mitigations, N-best decoding and phonetic entity correction, leave most of the gap intact, which points at retrieval structure rather than transcription surface form. We release the code and a transcript dataset covering 3 multi-hop QA benchmarks x 4 English accents (3,000 questions, 12,000 rows).
